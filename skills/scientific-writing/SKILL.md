# Scientific Writing

Draft or revise the requested text from supplied, traceable evidence. Preserve claim strength, uncertainty, study design, methods, units, denominators, and population or time-point definitions. Keep relevant null and adverse findings. Flag missing or unverified facts, citations, or declarations without filling gaps with plausible details.

## Choose the level of work

- **Language edit.** Edit the requested passage for clarity and preserve its meaning, numbers, and citations. Flag scientific ambiguities for the author. A language-only edit needs no evidence registry, policy search, or audit scaffold.
- **Evidence-based draft or substantive revision.** Identify the sources and supplied results needed for the requested section. Use [writing principles](references/writing_principles.md) for reporting choices and [evidence provenance](references/evidence_workflow.md) when a claim registry or source verification is needed. For a formal revision response, use the [reviewer-response workflow](references/writing_principles.md#revision-responses).
- **Formal manuscript or submission audit.** Use [evidence provenance](references/evidence_workflow.md) for claim-to-source mapping and human verification, [reporting guidelines](references/reporting_guidelines.md) for design-specific coverage, and [journal policies](references/journal_policies.md) for current venue requirements. Check methods, results, displays, and citations for consistency. Use the [local audit tools](references/cli_reference.md) as relevant. Keep unresolved items visible for the authors' final approval.

Before drafting or substantively revising a manuscript for a specified venue, check the current [AI-use rules](references/journal_policies.md) for its article type. Proceed with the forms of assistance the policy permits and flag unresolved permission before producing venue-directed text.

When the deliverable calls for them, consult the guidance on [figures and tables](references/figures_tables.md), [citation styles](references/citation_styles.md), or [report formatting](references/professional_report_formatting.md).

## Confidentiality and accountability

Keep confidential materials within the authorized environment. External processing of unpublished manuscripts, reviewer material, restricted data, or personal information requires explicit authorization and documented review of applicable policies. Use the minimum necessary information. See [authorship, AI, and confidentiality](references/authorship_ai_confidentiality.md) for formal disclosures, consent, and human approval. Accountable authors control scientific decisions, disclosures, and submission.
